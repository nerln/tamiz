Sources

Tamiz's data comes from public sources. Here is where it comes from and how it is cited.

This site is not an official source. The official text is the one published in the BOE, in the DOGV or on the body's own website.

Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE)

Citation

Source of the data: Agencia Estatal Boletín Oficial del Estado. www.boe.es

When a piece of data is taken from the BOE (for example, a summary), it is cited as: “Based on data from the Agencia Estatal Boletín Oficial del Estado”.

The consolidated legislation is a consolidated text for information purposes only.

National Database of Grants (BDNS)

Citation

Origin of the data: Intervención General de la Administración del Estado. www.infosubvenciones.es

The BDNS is used to find calls. We read the requirements in the official text of each call. Tamiz does not use the names of the beneficiaries: only the calls.

The BDNS legal notice is on its legal notice page. Read it there before reusing its data.

Sources of each card

Each card links to its source and, when it is another page, to its official site. The cards are grouped by area. The reading dates are on each card: always check them at the source.
