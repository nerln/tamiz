Alerts: get told about deadlines

Tamiz does not send you alerts and does not keep your email. If you want to be told when a call comes out, sign up yourself to these official services.

You give your email to the official service, not to us. Tamiz has no newsletter, no alerts of its own and no feed of news.

Official services

Mi BOE: grant alert

It sends you every day the rules and calls for scholarships, prizes and other aid. It is the first one we recommend.

You sign up yourself, with your email and a password, on the Boletín Oficial del Estado. It is free and asks you to confirm your email.

Information about the service on boe.es

Empléate (SEPE)

You can create alerts so that job offers reach you by email and/or SMS. Check on the SEPE website which steps and which registration they require.

The offers come from companies and organisations: they are not rights or aid.

Job offers on sepe.es

EURAXESS

Opportunities for researchers in Europe. You need a “My EURAXESS” account, which is free. Look on the website for how to create alerts.

EURAXESS on euraxess.ec.europa.eu
