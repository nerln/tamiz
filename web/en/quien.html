About

Tamiz is a personal project: free, non-profit and without advertising.

What it is

A simple way to find out which aid, grants and procedures in Spain and in the Comunitat Valenciana could fit you, and where to apply for them.

Why it exists

Aid is spread across different bulletins and official sites, and written in difficult language. Many people do not apply for what they could get because they do not know it exists. Tamiz wants to shorten that path.

How it is made

  • Each aid is summarised from its official call, with a link to the source.
  • The results are calculated by the program, comparing your answers with the requirements of each aid.
  • Your answers do not leave your browser.

Tamiz was also made with the help of artificial intelligence. The information is indicative: always check the official source.

What it is not

Tamiz is not a public body and is not linked to any administration. It is not legal, tax or administrative advice.
